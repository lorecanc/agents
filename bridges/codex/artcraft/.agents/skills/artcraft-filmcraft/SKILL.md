# FilmCraft

Use `sequence_inspect` to identify the sequence and target track. Check the frame rate and
source range before cutting. Decide whether later clips should move or keep their timecodes.
After the edit inspect the cut and downstream sync, then preview frames on both sides of the
boundary. Use the sequence's timecode or frame coordinates where the command accepts them.

When placing a rendered composition, confirm its frame rate, duration and first frame against
the sequence. Check the outgoing transition as well as the incoming one.
