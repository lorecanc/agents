# SoundCraft

Use `inspect_session` to locate the passage by track, clip or marker and check its routing.
Choose a time basis that matches the edit, such as samples for a splice or bars and beats for a
musical cue. Check the session rate or tempo before converting. After a move or trim, verify
clip boundaries and routing, and audition the transition when playback is available. For a
desktop-only operation, check which session the bridge controls.

For a mix export, verify the chosen time range includes the end of the decay. Check the intended
output bus and audition the exported section when available.
